Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for parents, shoppers, partners, and families exploring Nappilos products, stories, delivery, and support.

What is Nappilos?

Nappilos is a kids brand built around safe play, character-led stories, learning products, books, and family-friendly shopping experiences.

Where can I shop?

The main Nappilos website shows products and the cart flow. Checkout and future product pages can be connected to shop.nappilos.com.

Are products safe for children?

Our product direction focuses on child-safe materials, age-appropriate design, and responsible manufacturing partners. Final product listings should always show exact safety details.

Do you have books and learning pages?

Yes. Books will live at books.nappilos.com, learning resources at learn.nappilos.com, and education programs at education.nappilos.com.

How do I contact support?

You can email support@nappilos.com or use the contact links in the website footer for help with products, orders, and partnerships.

Can I become a reseller?

Yes. Visit the Reseller Hub page to learn the basic direction. More reseller forms and requirements can be added later.
