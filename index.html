About Nappilos - India's Premier Kids Brand

Nappilos LLP is India's first multi-product kids brand, revolutionizing the baby and children's product market with premium organic cotton muslin products. Founded in 2026, Nappilos serves families across India with two specialized divisions: Baby Nappilos for ages 0-3 years and Kids Nappilos for ages 3-14 years.

Baby Nappilos - Premium Baby Products India (0-3 Years)

Baby Nappilos offers comprehensive newborn and infant care products made from 100% organic cotton muslin. Our baby product range includes:

  • Baby Muslin Swaddles - Breathable, chemical-free swaddle blankets for newborns
  • Baby Jabla (Knotted & Snap Button) - Soft baby tops with easy closures
  • Baby Nappies (Knot Type) - Reusable cloth diapers, eco-friendly alternative
  • Baby Shorts - Comfortable bottoms with elastic waistbands
  • Baby Night Suits - 2-piece sleepwear for peaceful sleep
  • Baby Frocks (Knot Type & Back Button) - Adorable dresses for little princesses
  • Baby Front Open Frock - Easy dressing design for convenience
  • Cap, Mittens & Booties Sets - Complete newborn accessory sets
  • Baby Cradle (Thottil) - Traditional hanging cradle with modern safety
  • Muslin Wipes - Reusable, eco-friendly baby wipes
  • Muslin Hooded Towel - Super absorbent bath towels with hood
  • Muslin Co-Ord Sets - Matching top and shorts outfits
  • Essential Gift Sets - 5-piece organic cotton gift boxes for baby showers
  • Premium Muslin Swaddles 3-Pack - Multi-design swaddle sets
