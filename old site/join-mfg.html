Manufacturing partners

Join MFG for white label and private label opportunities

This page is for manufacturers who want to work with Nappilos on future product development, white label production, private label production, packaging, and supply partnerships.

Required compliance mindset

Manufacturers must follow all applicable government rules, safety regulations, labor requirements, tax requirements, product standards, and documentation rules for their category and operating region.

Documents we may request

  • Business registration and tax documents
  • Factory or production unit details
  • Product safety and testing certificates
  • Material declarations and sourcing details
  • Quality control process documents
  • Export, import, or shipping documents if applicable

Product expectations

We care about child safety, reliable materials, clean finishing, responsible packaging, consistent quality, and honest product information. Any children product must be suitable for its intended age group.

How contact starts

Manufacturers can introduce their company, product categories, MOQ, lead times, certifications, sample availability, and whether they support white label, private label, or custom development.

Important note

Nappilos will review manufacturing opportunities carefully. Submitting interest does not guarantee approval. Final onboarding should include document verification, sample review, quality checks, and written agreement terms.
